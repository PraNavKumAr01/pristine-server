The history of the internet dates back to the 1960s when the first concepts of a global network were developed. Initially a project by the U.S. Department of Defense, the internet has grown exponentially to become a fundamental part of modern life. The development of the World Wide Web in the 1990s made the internet accessible to the general public, revolutionizing communication, commerce, and information sharing. Today, the internet continues to evolve, shaping the way we live, work, and connect with each other.
